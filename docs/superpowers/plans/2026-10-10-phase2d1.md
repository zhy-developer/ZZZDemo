One Undo transaction per target; source is read-only, so no untracked cross-asset mutation.

Final review ruling: the initial phrase "contiguous reflow" was too broad. Nonadjacent copied animation segments, or mixed selections whose earliest event precedes the first copied animation, can leave animation gaps. Preserve relative timing rather than packing clips independently of their events: reject the complete transaction with the Validator's ANIMATION_CONTIGUITY diagnostic. This is a documented paste limitation; no silent timing rewrite. Read-only final review found no additional high-confidence atomicity, identity, reference-retention or Undo defect.
